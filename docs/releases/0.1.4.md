# Sleeper Draft Assistant 0.1.4

This is a stable release following 0.1.3. The first things you will notice are
that draft preparation is now one short checklist with one button for the next
step, **Manage data** opens by saying what your week is missing, and Settings
keeps its local-data controls behind one line.

The data format is unchanged, and 0.1.3 can read data written by 0.1.4 (see
[Rollback](#rollback)). Releases before 1.0 may change stored data or behavior
between versions. Close 0.1.3 and back up the complete app-data directory before
upgrading.

## Behavior changes to know about

- **A CSV that matched 0 players no longer completes a preparation step.** This
  applies to ECR rankings, season projections and ADP. If an unfinished draft
  already has such a file, Draft preparation opens and asks you to replace or
  clear it. The file itself stays stored until you do. Any match above 0 still
  counts, so a partial set of projections still passes. In a live draft, **Open
  emergency board only** is still offered.
  See [#94](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/94).
- **Enter draft room appears only when you are ready.** It was a disabled button.
  Until every step is done, the row says "Opens when the four steps are done".
  See [#92](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/92).
- **Completed drafts open for review with data missing.** In Draft preparation a
  completed draft is headed "Draft is over" and its button is **View draft
  results**. The button used to stay disabled when data was missing.
  See [#92](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/92).
- **Support report and full reset moved.** **Support report** and **Delete all
  local app data** are now inside the **Local data** line in Settings, which
  starts closed.
  See [#95](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/95).
- **Clear week clears the week its line reports.** It used to clear whatever was
  typed in the Week field, which could be a different week.
  See [#93](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/93).

## Changes

### Draft preparation

See [#92](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/92) for
this section.

- One list replaces the header and cards: ECR rankings, season projections, ADP,
  Assistant, then Draft room.
- One green button names the next step, such as **Upload PPR ECR CSV** or
  **Confirm Assistant settings**.
- The heading counts what is left, for example "Two steps before the draft room".
  It reads "Checking the Assistant" until the Assistant's status is known.
- Finished rows collapse to one line of facts and open for replace, clear and the
  source link. Any row can be opened early to upload out of order.
- A warning, old optional data or 0 matched players turns a collapsed row's facts
  amber.
- All Assistant controls are behind **Change settings**.
- When a step finishes, keyboard focus moves to the next step's button. The
  emergency board focuses its heading, and **Finish draft setup** hands focus back
  to the next step.

### Manage data

See [#93](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/93) for
this section.

- The drawer leads with what the selected week is missing, with at most one main
  action and FantasyPros links. **Weekly projections**, **Season value rankings**
  and **Coverage** are one line each and open on demand.
- The lead states the week actually loaded, not a week requested or typed. Files
  chosen in the lead import into that week.
- A green dot means the week's files are loaded on Sleeper's active week and
  nothing has failed. Old files, another season, saved files from another week,
  a load in progress, a rejected import for the week on screen and a failed
  refresh replace the "loaded" heading.
- A week you chose to view can still say its projections are loaded, with a
  notice that you're viewing that week and no green dot. A validation, file-read
  or clear error removes the green dot while it's showing, and the heading can
  stay, because the saved files didn't change.
- While the app stays open, an import the app rejected is remembered for its
  league, season and week until an import or clear for that week succeeds.
- The Week and Season fields follow the view when it moves, such as after **Back
  to Week N**.
- **Coverage** shows every readiness warning instead of the first three. The
  scoring reminder moved to **Weekly projections**.

### Week loads and keyboard focus

See [#94](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/94) for
this section.

- A failed load of another week now keeps your team, the loaded week and the Manage
  data drawer on screen, and shows the error in the drawer. The Week field returns
  to the loaded week. There is one exception under
  [Requirements and limitations](#requirements-and-limitations).
- Closing Settings no longer scrolls the page.
- Saving Assistant settings keeps focus in the drawer when the Save button becomes
  disabled.
- When focus has fallen back to the page, clicking page text no longer makes Tab
  skip the control next to what you clicked.

### Settings and home

See [#95](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/95) for
this section.

- **Local data** is one closed line showing your total saved records. Every row,
  count, clear action and confirmation is inside it. If the counts fail to load,
  the closed line says so in amber.
- The Assistant section now starts with its status, then Provider. No field or
  saved value changed.
- The status reads **Assistant ready**, **Assistant needs attention** or
  **Assistant off**. Off is no longer amber.
- The home page has a new line: "Draft and lineup help for Sleeper. The app stores
  its data on this device; Assistant requests go through your Codex account."
- The home card keeps one width when an error appears, and opening a draft from a
  scrolled home page lands at the top of the workspace.

### Release process

- The every-release Windows smoke now names two separate checks by a person: a
  short first run on a temporary local Windows account, including what the app
  says when Codex is missing, and the full interactive upgrade smoke.
  See [#94](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/94).

## Tested platforms

Windows 11 Pro 26H2 (build 26300.9711) is the only platform the release checks
use. A release is published only after two human checks pass on its exact
commit: a first run on a temporary Windows account and the full interactive
smoke on an upgraded profile. CI's automated Windows package smoke doesn't
replace either one. Linux packaging and CI do not establish support on other
Windows versions or operating systems.

## Install safely

Artifacts are **not code-signed**, and Windows SmartScreen may warn that the
publisher is unknown. Download only from this repository's Releases page and
check each file's SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before running it. See
[INSTALLING.md](../INSTALLING.md). Third-party licenses are in
`resources\THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

## Requirements and limitations

- Sleeper is the only supported fantasy platform. Access is tokenless and
  read-only. The app does not submit picks, change lineups or modify your
  Sleeper account.
- You supply your own ranking and projection exports. No third-party ranking
  data is bundled.
- The normal assistant workflow requires a locally installed, signed-in Codex
  CLI, which the app runs through its supported app-server integration. During
  an active draft, board-only recovery remains available when setup or Codex is
  unavailable.
- AI advice is advisory; check it yourself. News lookups can still time out or
  find no recent reporting. Citation dates are reported by the model and are
  checked for format, recency and consistency with the link, but not against
  the publisher's page.
- Automatic Assistant calls in the draft room skip live news and use the status
  metadata Sleeper supplies. Typed questions and **Retry** can still check news
  when Web search is on.
- Custom scoring and TE-premium accuracy depend on imports that match your
  league. IDP and auction drafts are unsupported. Dynasty and keeper valuation
  is not modeled. The app handles keeper pick order but does not value keepers.
- Linear, third-round-reversal and keeper order have not been tested on a live
  draft in progress.
- Sleeper's injury status is general player information, not specific to the
  week you are viewing.
- If the team fails to load after you switch league or draft, the team disappears
  and the app shows the draft view with no error message. To retry, reopen the
  draft from the switcher.
- If you typed a roster value that differs from Sleeper's roster ID, such as a
  slot, a failed week load removes the team the same way, with no error message.
  Choosing a league from your account uses the real roster ID and is not affected.
- If the reload after a rankings import or clear fails, **Weekly projections** can
  show "Week N didn't update" even though you asked for no week and the import or
  clear succeeded. The same can follow a season projection import or a weekly
  clear. The team stays on screen with a failed-refresh notice, and data you just
  cleared can stay visible until a refresh succeeds.
- After you press a clear button in **Local data**, or **Cancel** or **Confirm**
  after it, keyboard focus leaves that control. The next Tab goes to **Close
  settings**, not to a nearby row.
- After a failed import for another week, the header can show the requested week
  while the lineup and attention box still show the earlier one. A question sent
  then uses the requested week.
- Automated tests of the redesigned screens used synthetic data.
- The app shows no pick timer, because it does not read one from Sleeper.
- The app has a dark theme only.
- Research threads disable Codex's built-in shell, browser, computer-use, app
  and plugin tools. They do not disable MCP servers you have configured in your
  own Codex setup.
- The app is not code-signed. See [Install safely](#install-safely).

## Local data and removal

Data normally lives under `%APPDATA%\Sleeper Draft Assistant`, with the database
in its `data` directory. The **Local data** line in Settings offers per-category
clears and **Delete all local app data**. The uninstaller keeps local data. Close
the app before deleting files by hand: delete `data` to remove your records, or
delete the whole directory to also remove renderer preferences.

## Rollback

The data format is unchanged. No API, storage, shared-schema or desktop code
changed between 0.1.3 and 0.1.4, the database schema version is still 1, and the
app saves the same browser preferences. 0.1.3 can read data written by 0.1.4 and
no restore is required. To roll back:

1. Close 0.1.4.
2. Reinstall 0.1.3 from its original release.

If you would rather return to exactly what you had before upgrading, restore
your backup between those steps. That discards anything imported or saved since.
Never restore a backup while either version is running.

If you keep your 0.1.4 data, one thing behaves differently in 0.1.3: a rankings,
season projection or ADP file that matched 0 players counts as complete again in
Draft preparation.
