# Sleeper Draft Assistant 0.1.3

This is a stable release following 0.1.1. It rebuilds the draft room around a
ranked shortlist near your turn and the board between turns, rebuilds Team
Manager around whether your lineup needs attention, makes Assistant answers
easier to read, and corrects who-picks-when in linear, third-round-reversal,
traded-pick and keeper drafts.

0.1.2 was never published: its release build failed, so there were no downloads.
Everything planned for it is included here, and upgrades go from 0.1.1 straight
to 0.1.3.

The data format is unchanged, and 0.1.1 can read data written by 0.1.3 (see
[Rollback](#rollback)). Releases before 1.0 may change stored data or behavior
between versions. Close 0.1.1 and back up the complete app-data directory before
upgrading.

## Changes

### Draft room

- Automatic Assistant calls skip live news to stay inside a pick clock and use
  supplied Sleeper status metadata. Typed questions and **Retry** still check
  news when material and Web search is enabled. Model time can still exceed a
  short pick clock.
- The demo holds on your pick for 45 seconds before its scripted selection;
  other teams still pick every 4.5 seconds.
- Closing a team roster after live picks remove its board opener returns focus
  to the question composer. A usable opener still receives focus.

You still make your picks in Sleeper. See
[#87](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/87) for this
section unless another pull request is linked.

- A slim pick strip says when you are on the clock or how many picks away you
  are.
- Near your turn, the room leads with one ranked shortlist in the Assistant's
  order, and the top row has a one-line reason. On the clock it is headed
  "Assistant's shortlist". One or two picks away, and before the draft starts,
  it is headed "Targets if available".
- Between turns, and whenever pick timing is unknown, the board leads. Below it
  are a Watching line when the Assistant has targets, your starter slots and the
  question box.
- When a shortlist player is picked, the row is struck through with "Taken by"
  and the team name. The next player moves up in the Assistant's own order and
  is labelled as its next choice while a new call loads. The list stays on
  screen. If everyone on it is gone, it points you to Find player.
- An answer overtaken by a pick is now shown instead of dropped. The heading
  names the pick it came from, and players already taken are struck through. An
  overtaken answer is never stored as your draft plan or in decision history.
- Find player and Plan are available as soon as you enter a live draft, before
  the first strategy arrives. They are not offered in board-only recovery.
- Recent picks and your roster are now in one **Picks and roster** drawer,
  available in every live draft view.
- The board is now a table for screen readers, and a real pick change is
  announced once. See [#84](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/84).

### Pick order fixes

- Traded picks: unpicked board cells now name the team that owns the pick.
- Linear drafts were treated as snake, which gave wrong turn counts. They now
  use linear order on the board, in the pick strip and in what the Assistant
  sees.
- Third-round-reversal drafts now use reversal order, read from Sleeper's
  `reversal_round` setting. Sleeper does not document that setting. If reported
  picks contradict the computed order, the strip says "Pick timing unknown". The
  board still uses a snake layout and marks a current cell in that case, so its
  team and pick placement may be wrong. A missing or wrong reversal setting can
  give incorrect timing until a contradicting pick is reported.
- Linear and reversal order were checked against synthetic drafts and completed
  public drafts, not a live draft in progress.
- The board no longer labels a pick "On the clock" before the draft starts.
  See [#84](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/84).
- Keepers: pre-filled keeper picks no longer push the current pick forward, and
  "picks away" no longer counts filled keeper slots. This was tested with
  synthetic drafts only, not a live in-progress keeper draft.
  See [#85](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/85).

### Team Manager

You still change your lineup in Sleeper. See
[#89](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/89) for this
section unless another pull request is linked.

- Team Manager opens on an attention box headed, for example, "2 things need a
  look". It lists starters who are on bye, have a Sleeper status or have an
  unknown bye status, as well as empty slots and gaps in your lineup data. A
  starter item goes to that row, and a data item opens the right control in
  **Manage data**.
- The box reports facts only. It quotes Sleeper's status as "listed", never says
  a player is playing and never recommends a player. Only starters count.
- It says "Lineup looks set" only when no starter or data item is raised for
  Sleeper's active week. That message names the facts it rests on, for example
  "9 starters, no byes or status flags, all projected for Week 8".
- Other states say what they are. Preseason and postseason show one neutral note.
  Otherwise, a week other than Sleeper's active one, a failed refresh or no
  starting slots shows a neutral note only when no attention items were raised.
  An unknown week raises an item that opens the week field.
- The lineup is a table with Slot, Player, Week and Status columns, plus Proj
  when at least one rostered player has a projection. A flagged row is tinted and
  has an Ask control that puts a question about that player in the question box
  without sending it. Ask is hidden when the Assistant is unavailable.
- Bench, injured reserve and taxi sit behind one row that starts closed.
- The Assistant is one line below the lineup, with Lineup, Waivers and Plan
  shortcuts (Depth replaces Lineup outside the regular season when no starting
  slot is empty). The thread has a
  fixed height, so the lineup stays first on the page, and a Conversation control
  hides it.
- Market activity is one line, shown only when there is activity.
- A weekly file that matched no players no longer counts as ready for that
  position. The attention box and **Manage data** say whether no players matched
  or the file had no rows, and the Assistant is told the same.
  See [#81](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/81).

### What moved or was removed

In the draft room ([#87](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/87)):

- Reasons, risks, evidence, news sources, confidence, turn timing and
  contingencies moved to **Why this call** on the shortlist's top row.
- The preference control moved to the top row, and clearing saved preferences
  moved to Find player.
- Next-turn and can-wait priorities moved to **Plan**.
- Near your turn, the board opens from a **Board** control in the question box.
  Full board is still in the board's header.
- Suggested questions appear when you move into the question box with the
  keyboard, and Ask appears once you type.

In Team Manager ([#89](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/89)):

- Readiness details moved to **Manage data**. That covers coverage, confidence,
  match rate and position status, which no longer appear with the roster.
- Manage team data is now **Manage data** in the top bar. The source-count
  tooltip and the preseason "Data: n/2 sources" pill are gone, and the weekly
  panel shows the source count.
- The three prompt cards are gone. Lineup, Waivers and Plan replace them.
- The in-thread shortcut chips (Matchup, Bench depth, Add/drop, Roster plan and
  Trending) are gone. You can still type any of those questions.
- Enter now sends and Shift+Enter adds a line, as in the draft room.
- Screen readers no longer hear each 60-second refresh, only a failed one.

### Assistant conversations

- Answers now render tables, numbered lists, bold, italics and inline code.
  Links are still limited to the same approved sites.
  See [#74](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/74) and
  [#76](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/76).
- Threads follow the newest message unless you have scrolled up to read.
- A Cancel button replaces Ask while an answer is pending. If the Assistant had
  already started, it finishes in the background and its answer isn't shown.
  Cancelled and failed questions are left out of follow-up history sent by the
  app, though the provider's own thread may still remember them.
  See [#77](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/77).

### Wording, screens and consistency

- The AI is now called the Assistant throughout. "Codex" remains where it names
  the program you install and configure. Internal status codes are replaced by
  readable labels, and error messages say what to do next. An unknown Sleeper
  username now gets a clear message.
  See [#78](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/78).
- A new visual system brings calmer type, consistent colors and better contrast
  for input borders and spinners.
  See [#75](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/75).
- All slide-out panels share one drawer, with a fixed header and the same width
  and phone layout.
  See [#79](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/79).
- Preparation has one primary button that takes you to the next unmet step.
  Projections and ADP now say "Required", as they always were for entering the
  draft room. File pickers are restyled and keep keyboard use and pasting.
  See [#83](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/83).
- A completed league remembers Team Manager, so reloading no longer flashes the
  board. See [#84](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/84).

### Testing

- The test suite now also runs on Windows in CI, which would have caught the
  failure that stopped the 0.1.2 build.
  See [#88](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/88).

## Tested platforms

Windows 11 Pro 26H2 (build 26300.9711) is the only tested platform. A release is
published only after the every-release real Windows smoke passes on its exact
commit. Linux packaging and CI do not establish support on other Windows
versions or operating systems.

## Install safely

Artifacts are **not code-signed**, and Windows SmartScreen may warn that the
publisher is unknown. Download only from this repository's Releases page and
check each file's SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before running it. See
[INSTALLING.md](../INSTALLING.md). Third-party licenses are in
`resources\THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

## Requirements and limitations

- Sleeper is the only supported fantasy platform. Access is tokenless and
  read-only. The app does not submit picks, change lineups or modify your
  Sleeper account.
- You supply your own ranking and projection exports. No third-party ranking
  data is bundled.
- The normal assistant workflow requires a locally installed, signed-in Codex
  CLI, which the app runs through its supported app-server integration. During
  an active draft, board-only recovery remains available when setup or Codex is
  unavailable.
- AI advice is advisory; check it yourself. News lookups can still time out or
  find no recent reporting. Citation dates are reported by the model and are
  checked for format, recency and consistency with the link, but not against
  the publisher's page.
- Custom scoring and TE-premium accuracy depend on imports that match your
  league. IDP and auction drafts are unsupported. Dynasty and keeper valuation
  is not modeled. The app handles keeper pick order but does not value keepers.
- Linear, third-round-reversal and keeper order have not been tested on a live
  draft in progress.
- Sleeper's injury status is general player information, not specific to the
  week you are viewing.
- After a failed import for another week, the header can show the requested week
  while the lineup and attention box still show the earlier one. A question sent
  then uses the requested week.
- Automated tests of the redesigned screens used synthetic data.
- The app shows no pick timer, because it does not read one from Sleeper.
- The app has a dark theme only.
- Research threads disable Codex's built-in shell, browser, computer-use, app
  and plugin tools. They do not disable MCP servers you have configured in your
  own Codex setup.

## Local data and removal

Data normally lives under `%APPDATA%\Sleeper Draft Assistant`, with the database
in its `data` directory. Settings offers per-category clears and **Delete all
local app data**. The uninstaller keeps local data. Close the app before
deleting files by hand: delete `data` to remove your records, or delete the
whole directory to also remove renderer preferences.

## Rollback

The data format is unchanged, so 0.1.1 can read data written by 0.1.3 and no
restore is required. To roll back:

1. Close 0.1.3.
2. Reinstall 0.1.1 from its original release.

If you would rather return to exactly what you had before upgrading, restore
your backup between those steps. That discards anything imported or saved since.
Never restore a backup while either version is running.

If you keep your 0.1.3 data, 0.1.1 behaves differently in three small ways:

- A weekly file that matched no players or had no rows counts as loaded for that
  position, and can read as ready, until you import weekly projections again.
- The Assistant's short reason lines are not shown. They were written alongside
  the full summary, which 0.1.1 still shows.
- **Delete all local app data** in 0.1.1 leaves the Team Manager preference that
  0.1.3 saves for completed leagues, which contains the league ID. To remove it,
  close the app and delete the whole directory.
