# Sleeper Draft Assistant 0.1.1

This is a stable patch release following 0.1.0. It improves news citations,
answers your questions sooner while the assistant is busy, and makes Team
Manager aware of bye weeks. The database schema is unchanged, but saved decision
history can now contain citations from sites that 0.1.0 does not accept (see
[Rollback](#rollback)). Releases before 1.0 may change stored data or behavior
between versions. Close 0.1.0 and back up the complete app-data directory before
upgrading.

## Changes

- **News citations come from more sources and carry real dates.** News lookups
  can now cite all 32 official NFL team sites and NBC Sports (including
  ProFootballTalk), as well as NFL.com and ESPN. All of them are on an
  exact-host allowlist, and their links open in your default browser. The
  research prompt asks for the article's own publication date. The app accepts
  only a valid date from the last seven days that agrees with any date in the
  link. Rolling injury
  reports, rosters, depth charts, transaction lists, player profiles and news
  index pages can't be cited by themselves. A source is rejected when its link
  contains a date that contradicts the reported date. Copy diagnostics now
  counts why sources were rejected (for example a missing or stale date, a
  rejected host, or an undated page), without recording links, titles or
  player names.
  See [#69](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/69)
  and [#70](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/70).
- **Questions no longer wait behind background draft-plan refreshes.** The
  assistant still runs one AI request at a time. Your draft and Team Manager
  questions, and an explicit Retry, now go ahead of queued automatic
  refreshes. Repeated refreshes for the same draft merge into one, and a
  refresh whose board changed while it waited is skipped. A request that is
  already running is not interrupted.
  See [#72](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/72).
- **Progress shows what the assistant is doing.** While you wait, the status
  reads "Waiting for another AI request…", "Thinking…" or "Checking news…".
  Copy diagnostics includes how long each stage of recent requests took, with
  no request content.
  See [#72](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/72).
- **Team Manager knows about byes.** For the selected regular-season week it
  shows each player's opponent and home or away status, and marks players on
  bye with a **BYE** tag. Codex is told that a player on bye scores zero that
  week. Bye players no longer count as missing weekly projections.
  - The schedule comes from Sleeper's tokenless, read-only
    `api.sleeper.app/schedule` endpoint. Sleeper does not document this
    endpoint, so if it is unavailable, Team Manager still loads, with an "NFL
    schedule unavailable" note.
  - When the schedule can't be loaded, the bye week from your imported draft
    rankings is used where one exists.
  - Players without a current NFL team, and preseason and postseason views,
    show no weekly bye facts.

  See [#71](https://github.com/itsreverence/sleeper-draft-assistant/pull/71).

## Tested platforms

This release has been tested only on Windows 11 Pro 26H2 (build 26300.9711).
It is tagged only after the every-release real Windows smoke passes on its exact
commit. Linux packaging and CI do not establish support on other Windows
versions or operating systems.

## Install safely

Artifacts are **not code-signed**, and Windows SmartScreen may warn that the
publisher is unknown. Download only from this repository's Releases page and
check each file's SHA-256 against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before running it. See
[INSTALLING.md](../INSTALLING.md). Third-party licenses are in
`resources\THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.

## Requirements and limitations

- Sleeper is the only supported fantasy platform. Access is tokenless and
  read-only. The app does not submit picks, change lineups or modify your
  Sleeper account.
- You supply your own ranking and projection exports. No third-party ranking
  data is bundled.
- The normal assistant workflow requires a locally installed, signed-in Codex
  CLI, which the app runs through its supported app-server integration. During
  an active draft, board-only recovery remains available when setup or Codex is
  unavailable.
- AI advice is advisory; check it yourself. News lookups can still time out or
  find no recent reporting. Citation dates are reported by the model and are
  checked for format, recency and consistency with the link, but not against
  the publisher's page.
- Custom scoring and TE-premium accuracy depend on imports that match your
  league. IDP and auction drafts are unsupported. Dynasty and keeper valuation is
  not modeled.
- News research threads disable Codex's built-in shell, browser, computer-use,
  app and plugin tools. They do not disable MCP servers you have configured in
  your own Codex setup.

## Local data and removal

Data normally lives under `%APPDATA%\Sleeper Draft Assistant`, with the database
in its `data` directory. Settings offers per-category clears and **Delete all
local app data**. The uninstaller keeps local data. Close the app before
deleting files by hand: delete `data` to remove your records, or delete the
whole directory to also remove renderer preferences. The NFL schedule is cached
only in memory.

## Rollback

0.1.1 can save news citations from team sites and NBC Sports in decision
history. 0.1.0 rejects those citations and would report the whole decision
history as incompatible. To roll back:

1. Close 0.1.1.
2. Restore the backup you made before upgrading. If you have no backup, first
   open 0.1.1's Settings and clear **Decision history**.
3. Reinstall 0.1.0 from its original release.

Never restore a backup while either version is running.
